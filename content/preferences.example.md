# Learned preferences

**This file is written by `/refine` and by `/create-posts` when you push back on a
draft. It is also yours to edit by hand — delete anything you disagree with.**

Every rule records the feedback that produced it, so you can always see *why* PostCraft
believes something. If a rule is wrong, deleting the line is the whole fix.

`/create-posts` reads this **after** `content/content-doctrine.md`. Where the two
conflict, a rule here wins — the doctrine is what you planned, this is what you learned.

---

## How entries look

```markdown
## Voice
- 2026-03-04 — Never open with a rhetorical question.
  *From:* "stop starting everything with a question, it's exhausting"
```

Date, the rule as an instruction, and the raw feedback underneath. Keep the raw words:
paraphrasing drifts, and in six months the original phrasing is what tells you whether
the rule still means what you meant.
